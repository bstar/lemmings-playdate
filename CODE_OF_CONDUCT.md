# Code of conduct

This is a small project. The expectations are short.

Be civil. Discuss the code, not the person writing it. Assume the other party
is acting in good faith, and ask before assuming they are not.

Harassment, personal attacks, and unwelcome personal attention are not
acceptable in issues, pull requests, or any other project space.

Reviews should be specific and about the work. "This breaks the fatal fall
distance because it does not account for the landing step" is useful. "This is
sloppy" is not.

Conduct concerns will be handled privately where possible. The maintainer
may edit, lock, or remove contributions and may block accounts for behaviour
that persists after being raised.
